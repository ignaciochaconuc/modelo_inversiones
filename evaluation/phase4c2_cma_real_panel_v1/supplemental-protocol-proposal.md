## Gates before incorporating a case

1. Local custodian documents rights per source revision and field. Preserve the
   licence version, canonical URL, required attribution and exclusions. A footer
   offer alone is not a finding that all quoted/embedded material is licensed.
2. Verify provenance, sufficient exact text, publication, actual acquisition
   times and cutoff. Source assertions are reports, not independently proved
   world facts. A regulator's investigation is not a finding of infringement.
3. Check URL/content duplicates and overlap with prior development/evaluation
   using authorised local data only. If content checks cannot lawfully be made,
   preserve that uncertainty and do not certify independence. Record related
   matters and non-independent document clusters.
4. Preserve sufficient context for negation, stages and partial relations; no
   synthetic paraphrase, fabricated summary, inferred ticker or fake provider.
5. Record an evidence revision, exact Unicode fields, deterministic evidence
   IDs and content hashes. Select no semantic references during preparation.

## Blind human review after later authorization

Reuse the eight dimensions and quote/provenance conventions in
`evaluation/phase4c2_attribution_v1/REVIEWER_GUIDE.md`: corporate_identity,
participation, role, relationship, ticker_link, impact_support, event_state,
event_stage. Use a real-panel-specific version rather than editing the frozen
synthetic guide or forcing the 32-case importers to accept these candidates.

Prepare separate blind evidence and operator metadata. Do not deliver sampling
hypotheses, prior predictions or hidden expected answers to reviewers. Give
neutral security evidence only in a separate documented second pass; until
such evidence is available, ticker linkage may remain unresolved. Never use
contemporary corporate knowledge as undocumented historical registry evidence.

The original independent-human route uses two actual reviewers independent of
development, separately sealed annotations and a third adjudicator for
disagreements. Record identity, assistance, prior exposure, conflicts, actual
review timestamps, exact minimal spans and uncertainty. No identities, times
or agreement are prefilled. AI-assisted adjudication is a separate stratum and
is not authorised here. Availability of suitable human reviewers is pending.

Freeze authorised evidence and references with attributed decisions before
revealing any system outputs. A future model execution needs its own explicit
authorization, current profile, budget and retention controls. No production
output, benchmark or comparison is created by this proposal.

## Rights, retention and access

The candidate OGL permission covers only material actually offered within its
scope. Preserve attribution and licence links; claim no CMA endorsement. Do
not include excluded personal data, third-party annexes, images or logos merely
because they appear on GOV.UK. Record human recipients and restrict working
copies to the approved use. Rights to source storage and the project's TTL for
model responses are separate. No content retention period is invented here:
it must be documented before an evidence bundle is acquired/released.

The 24 Finnhub candidates remain in their original blocked state. No source
content is transferred to an external AI service, even if OGL might permit
such a use in principle. A URL-based AI retrieval is also a content transfer.

## Completion limits

Eight cases and the proposed coverage are not normative statistical minima.
The future panel must report actual adjudicated coverage and gaps, abstentions,
rejections, technical failures and nonconclusive judgments. A small panel does
not estimate population precision/recall or certify generalisation. No finding
of exposure, causal economic impact or returns follows from a named entity.

H02 remains unresolved. No integral pilot completion, temporal rehabilitation,
semantic-truth certification or official phase transition is authorised.
