# ADR-036: Proposed economic prompt v4 contract alignment

## Status

**Proposed**, not approved for production activation. Preparation state:
`phase4c2_p1_prompt_v4_proposed_pending_review`.
No provider call, new inference or Phase 4C.3 authorization.

## Proposal and boundary

Keep historical v3 prompt, selection/result/runtime contracts, resolver, semantic
validators and economic attribution byte-identical. Place draft1 of the proposed
news-interpretation-prompt-v4 outside src, with pinned sources/artifact hashes,
new fictional contract probes and a prompt–validator mismatch matrix.

Clarify lifecycle literals, quote/source namespaces and uniqueness, preservation
of qualifiers, numeric representation and separate identity/participation/impact.
Necessary lexical checks do not prove semantic support. Prefer honest unknown
or abstention over changing meaning to satisfy the guard. Do not retrospectively
adjudicate the 22 historical stage rejections from missing expired content.

This is not an accepted validator change. Morphological equivalents, negation
scope, temporal/event binding, numeric locale/boundaries, date expansion and
additional relation frames require separate versioned proposals and review.
No fuzzy matching, global normalizations, corporate alias expansion or automatic
conversion of prior rejections into accepted interpretations.

## Future activation prerequisites — not implemented

The existing runtime/results pin prompt-v3 and must not claim a v4 generation
under that identity. Review new version-bearing runtime/result/replay support,
wire/cache/provenance binding, compatibility and budget reservations before
connecting a new prompt. Keep the output selection schema v3 unless a separate
decision actually needs a schema change. Require current attestation, new plan,
P0 diagnostics and specific owner authorization for any later controlled run.

Offline tests assess handcrafted responses against unchanged validators, not
LLM adherence, accuracy or generalization. Preserve v3, all source evidence,
frozen references, historical attempts/ledger and original profiles. No use of
ChatGPT references for tuning, no official TEST and no Phase 4C.3.

See [P1 review and risks](../phase4c2-p1-prompt-validator-alignment.md).
